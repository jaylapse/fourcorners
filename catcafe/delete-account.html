Delete your CatCafe account

CatCafe by Four Corners Games · Last updated October 18, 2026

In short: you can delete your CatCafe account yourself, any time, from inside the game. If you no longer have the game and you used Google Play Games, email us and we will delete it for you.

Delete it in the game

  1. Open CatCafe, tap the menu button (top right) and tap Settings.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account, then tap it again to confirm.
  4. If you play with Google Play Games, confirm with it when asked.

Your account is deleted straight away, and the game starts a fresh bakery.

Or ask us by email

If you can’t open the game any more, write to support@fourcornersgames.ca with the subject “Delete my CatCafe account”, and tell us the Google Play Games name you play under so we can find your account. We will delete it and confirm within 30 days.

If you only played as a guest (without Google Play Games), your account isn’t linked to any name or email, so we have no way to tell which one is yours. Open the game and delete it from Settings as above. If the game is gone, the guest account holds only your bakery’s progress, with nothing that identifies you.

What is deleted

  • Your saved bakery: coins, Sprinkles, stars, recipes, stations, decorations, helpers, purchases and the rest of your progress.
  • Your login: your anonymous account and its link to Google Play Games.
  • The progress saved on the device you delete from.

What is kept, and for how long

We keep nothing about you after your account is deleted. Google Play keeps its own record of anything you bought (order ID, item, price, date and country) for accounting, tax and refunds, and information collected by Google AdMob is kept by Google under its own policies.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Bakery Club subscription bought through Google Play. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions).

Delete some data but keep your account

You can also ask us to delete part of your information without closing your account, for example to erase your saved bakery and start over. Write to support@fourcornersgames.ca with the Google Play Games name you play under and tell us what you want removed. We will do it and confirm within 30 days. To stop using Google Play Games with CatCafe, you don’t need to write: tap Sign Out in Settings → Account.

For everything else about your information, see our Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

CatCafe · Last updated October 18, 2026

In short: the first time you play, CatCafe makes an anonymous account for you so your bakery is saved online. We never ask for your name or email. On Android, Google Play Games can link to it so your bakery comes back on a new phone. Anything you buy is paid through Google Play: we never see your card or payment details. The Android version has optional ads from Google AdMob that you choose to watch for a reward; Google’s ad service uses your device’s advertising ID (see Ads). We never sell your data, and you can delete your account any time from Settings → Account.

This policy explains what personal information CatCafe collects, why, and what you can do about it. We are a small studio in Quebec and we have tried to keep it short and plain.

CatCafe is still in development. Ads and purchases are being added to the Android version; this policy already describes how they work, so it covers them from the day they arrive.

Who we are

CatCafe is made by Four Corners Games, run by Jay and Will in Quebec, Canada. Jay and Will are jointly the persons in charge of the protection of personal information. You can reach them at support@fourcornersgames.ca.

Who can play

CatCafe is for people aged 14 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 14. If you believe a child under 14 has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.

What we collect

When you play, the game creates a random anonymous account with Google Firebase the first time it opens. You don’t choose a name or give an email. Your bakery is saved with that account, so it can be restored on another device or after a reinstall. What is saved:

  • your progress: coins, Sprinkles, stars, recipes, stations and their levels, decorations, helpers, regulars, daily goals, the tutorial, and the rest of your bakery;
  • what you have bought, such as No Ads or how long your Bakery Club lasts;
  • when you last played and how long you have played in all;
  • the game version, and a random ID for each install of the game, so the game can tell your devices apart when it compares saves.

The game uploads this save every few minutes while it is open, and when you close it or switch apps. Only your account can read it.

If you play on Android with Google Play Games, the game signs you in with it automatically and links it to your account, so your bakery comes back on another phone. We receive your Google Play Games player ID, and Firebase keeps the public profile details that come with it (such as your display name) with your login; the game doesn’t use or show them. We do not receive your email address or your Google password. You can sign out from Settings → Account; your bakery carries on as a guest, and the game will not sign you in automatically again until you choose to.

Like any online service, Google Firebase, which we use to run accounts and cloud saves, receives technical information such as your IP address and device type when your device connects to it.

On your device, the game keeps a save file with your progress, your settings (volumes, notifications) and a login token that keeps you signed in. On the web version this lives in your browser’s storage. Reminders the game shows, for example when a new oven is ready, are scheduled on your device; nothing about them is sent to us, and you can turn them off in Settings or in your phone’s settings. We do not use cookies or analytics tools ourselves; the ads described under Ads are the only third-party service that uses your advertising ID.

If you buy something (Sprinkles, No Ads, the Bakery Club or another pack, in the Google Play version of the game), the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card number, bank details or any other payment information. Google Play gives the game a purchase record: the order ID, what you bought, when, and a purchase token. The game uses it to deliver your purchase, and keeps the token on your device until Google Play confirms the purchase is complete, so that no purchase is delivered twice. What you bought is then saved with your progress.

Why we collect it

  • to run your account and save your bakery online;
  • to restore your bakery on another device or after a reinstall, and to ask you which one to keep if two devices disagree;
  • to deliver what you buy and to help with questions about a purchase;
  • to show the optional ads you choose to watch and give you their rewards;
  • to answer you when you contact us.

We do not sell your information.

What other players can see

Nothing. CatCafe has no leaderboards, usernames or chat. Your bakery is private to your account.

Who we share it with

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) to store your account and saved bakery. Google acts as our service provider. Your saved bakery is stored in Google’s Montréal data centre region (Canada). Sign-in data handled by Firebase Authentication and Google Play Games may be processed by Google outside Quebec and outside Canada, in jurisdictions with different privacy rules. If you play a test version in your web browser, it is hosted on itch.io, which has its own privacy policy. Purchases are processed by Google Play under Google’s own terms and privacy policy; Google shares the order information described above with us and handles refunds. In the Android version, ads are provided by Google AdMob, which receives the information described under Ads and uses it under Google’s own privacy policy, including outside Quebec and outside Canada. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Ads

The Android version of CatCafe has optional rewarded ads: an ad only plays when you tap a button that says what you get for watching it (for example “Watch Ad: +25% coins” on the Welcome Back card, or finishing a wait early). There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt the game. The No Ads purchase gives you those rewards without watching ads. The web version has no ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. So that ads can be loaded and ready when you ask for one, Google’s ad software starts when the game opens. It collects, and shares with Google:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address;
  • how you interact with ads (for example whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped);
  • technical information such as device type, operating system, and crash and performance data from the ad software.

Google uses this to show ads, measure how they perform, and prevent fraud. We only ask for family-friendly ads. Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Google’s consent form appears the first time you open the game, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings. Elsewhere, ads may be personalized using your advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We do not receive your advertising ID or your ad data ourselves; Google only gives us overall reports (such as how many ads were shown). CatCafe does not use analytics tools.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and saved bakery for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (Settings → Account → Delete Account; with Google Play Games you may be asked to confirm with it), we delete your saved bakery, then your login, and your progress is wiped from your device. Information collected by Google AdMob is kept by Google under its own policies. Google Play also keeps a record of each purchase (order ID, item, price, date and country), which we can see in our Google Play Console for accounting, tax and refund purposes, for as long as Google retains it. Step by step, including how to ask us by email: Delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, give you a copy, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to support@fourcornersgames.ca. We will reply within 30 days. You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from the game. If we cannot resolve a concern, you can contact the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca).

Security

We rely on Google’s security for stored data and limit what we collect to what the game needs. No system is perfectly secure. If an incident is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Commission d’accès à l’information as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the game before they take effect.

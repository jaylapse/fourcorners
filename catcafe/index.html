CatCafe

A cozy cat bakery that never closes

Run a little bakery in a village full of cats. Bake croissants, brew coffee and hire helpers who keep the shop going, even while you’re away. Leave it open on your desk and watch your bakery grow.

In developmentAndroidEnglish & FrançaisNo forced ads
Follow along on Discord How it plays ↓

How it plays

Tap to bake when you’re there. Your helpers handle it when you’re not.

1

Bake and brew

  • Fill the display case with croissants, muffins, pies and more.
  • Brew coffee, lattes and cappuccinos to go with them.
  • Tap the oven to speed it up, then upgrade to a brick oven and beyond.
2

Hire helpers

  • Helper cats run the ovens and the coffee machine for you.
  • Your bakery keeps selling while the game is closed.
  • Come back to a Welcome Back card with what your helpers earned.
3

Make it yours

  • Decorate every wall, floor and window, and the shop gets cozier.
  • Open a lounge with café tables and a string quartet.
  • Earn stars, then open new bakeries in new towns.

Meet the regulars

Some cats come back every day. Each one has a favourite.

Keep it open on your desk. Something sparkly might stop by.

CatCafe is in development and coming to Google Play. Ads are only ever a button you choose to tap, for a reward it names first.

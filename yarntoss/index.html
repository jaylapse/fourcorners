Yarn Toss

Granny vs. the cats

Granny just wants some peace and quiet, but her house has been overrun by mischievous cats! Plant your slippers, grab a ball of yarn and toss it at every whiskered intruder before they reach you.

How to play

Tap to aim, Granny throws. The cats do the rest.

1

Defend every room

  • Tap to throw. Granny stands her ground: you aim, she throws.
  • Fight through the living room, kitchen, garage, yard, bedroom, bathroom, basement and attic.
  • Every room ends with a boss, like Sir Fluffington and the Drainpipe Demon.
2

Upgrade your yarn

  • Frosty yarn slows cats down.
  • Boomerang yarn pierces a whole line of cats and flies back.
  • Explosive yarn and Candy Shards add even more chaos.
3

Play your way

  • Casual: relaxed runs where you retry the room when you fall.
  • Ranked: every run counts toward the global leaderboard.
  • Achievements, outfits for Granny and cloud saves.

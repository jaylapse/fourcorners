Delete your YarnToss account

YarnToss by Four Corners Games · Last updated September 29, 2026

In short: you can delete your YarnToss account yourself, any time, from inside the game. If you no longer have the game, email us and we will delete it for you.

Delete it in the game

  1. Open YarnToss and tap Settings (the gear on the main menu).
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm with your password, or with Google Play Games if that is how you sign in.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Or ask us by email

If you can’t open the game any more, write to support@fourcornersgames.ca with the subject “Delete my YarnToss account”. Send it from the email address you signed up with, or tell us your YarnToss username if you sign in with Google Play Games, so we can find your account. We will delete it and confirm within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: username, game progress, best score, in-game currency, skins, achievements and any runs in progress.
  • Your entry on the leaderboards.
  • Your reserved username, so someone else can use it.
  • Your login: your email address and password, or your link to Google Play Games.
  • The progress saved on the device you delete from.

What is kept, and for how long

Only one thing: the record of any username changes (your internal account ID, the old and new usernames, and the date), so we can deal with abuse such as offensive names. It does not contain your email address. We keep it only as long as it is needed for moderation.

If you only played as a guest, there is no account to delete: your progress lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

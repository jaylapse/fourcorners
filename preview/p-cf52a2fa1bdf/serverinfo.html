Rules

Good to know

Experience is 2x, everything else is 1x, and the spawn rate is faster than usual.

The server is EU-hosted.

Daily serversave at 07:00 server time – logins are blocked for the five minutes before it, and the server is back about a minute later.

Botting is allowed and you may play up to 3 accounts.

Premium days and shop items are bought under Item Shop and Premium Days.

No wipe is planned – your progress stays.

Found a bug? Report it to GM BubbaGame in-game or on Discord.

Questions

FAQ

Which client should I use?

7.72 is the original experience and is recommended if you play by hand. OTClient v8 has a bot, smart walking and WASD, and is the client for watching someone's cast. Choose a client →

Is botting allowed?

Yes. Botting is allowed and you may play up to 3 accounts.

Will the server be wiped?

No wipe is planned – your progress stays.

When is the serversave?

Daily at 07:00 server time. Logins are blocked for the five minutes before it, and the server is back about a minute later.

What are the rates?

Experience is 2x, everything else is 1x, and the spawn rate is faster than usual.

How do I get Premium?

Premium Days are ordered through the admin. See Donate.

My v8 map reset itself

The v8 client saves the map file when you log out, so a crash or a lost connection during the daily server restart can overwrite it. Keep a copy of the .otmm file somewhere safe and just paste it back if your map is gone. Full details →

The game stutters

Try the PC settings in Performance tips.

Where do I find tasks?

At any post office NPC. !tasklist shows every kill task; !task shows your current one.

How do I stream or watch?

!cast streams your gameplay to other players; use OTClient v8 to watch someone's cast.
